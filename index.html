Manila Nature Spots

A simple guide to peaceful nature spots around Manila where you can relax, explore, and take a break from the busy city.

About Me

Jay Mella

I created this guide to share some of my recommended nature spots around Manila. I enjoy discovering places where people can relax, enjoy greenery, and take a break from the busy city environment.

Places I Recommend

These are three nature spots that I recommend for people who want to enjoy a peaceful outdoor experience.

🌿

Arroceros Forest Park

A peaceful urban forest in the heart of Manila. It is a great place to walk, relax, and enjoy the trees and greenery.

🌳

Rizal Park

A famous green space in Manila where visitors can walk, relax, enjoy the gardens, and spend time outdoors.

🍃

La Mesa Eco Park

A large natural area with trees, trails, and peaceful surroundings for people who enjoy being close to nature.
